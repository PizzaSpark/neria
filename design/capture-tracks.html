Capture, round two: Automatic, and a track per app

Two asks: an "Automatic" pick that records whatever game is fullscreen in front at that moment, and sound as separate tracks (track 1 everything, then each app on its own, to fix in your video editor or in Neria), with a list of apps that are never recorded, ever. Below: the options side by side, and the one Windows limit that shapes the sound part.

The limit

What Windows lets us do with app sound

  • One app's sound on its own: yes, for as many apps as we like (each is its own WASAPI capture, as Discord is today).
  • "Everything but one app": yes, in one go (today's "Game & PC (no Discord)").
  • "Everything but two or more apps": not in one go. Windows' capture can leave out one app only. With two or more in "Never record", Neria builds "everything" from each other app on its own, which means an app that starts playing after recording started isn't in it until Neria notices and restarts the tracks (every few seconds it checks; a restart drops what was kept before it, so it waits for a quiet moment: no save in the last 10 s).
  • Tracks are fixed while recording (a file can't grow a track halfway): a new app gets its own track at that restart too. Until then its sound is still in "Everything else".
1 · Automatic

Recording whatever game is in front

1A · "Automatic" is the first row of What to record PICK

It records the game that's fullscreen (or borderless) in front; with no game in front, the screen you're using. The row says what it's on right now. Switching to another game moves to it (what was kept of the old game is dropped: one buffer at a time keeps the GPU cost tiny). Alt-Tabbing to the desktop or Discord doesn't switch: it only follows games, and stays on the last one until another game comes to the front.

1B · A tick under the list: "Follow the game in front"

Same behaviour, but a tick on top of a radio list is two controls for one choice, and it's unclear what the picked row means while it's ticked. Not mocked further.

2 · Sound

Track 1 everything, then a track per app

2A · Each app on its own, automatically, with a "Never record" list PICK

No ticking per app: every app playing sound when it starts gets a track, named after it. Track 1 is always everything mixed (what any player plays), then the game, the others, "Everything else" (system sounds, apps that started later), and the mic last. Never record is a list you add apps to (from what's playing now, or any program); they're left out of every track, everything included, forever, and Settings keeps them. The list below the tracks shows what the next save will hold, with each one's level moving.

2B · You pick which apps get a track (a tick each), the rest stay in "Everything else"

Fewer tracks in the file and you choose, but a list of ticks for apps that come and go, and the app you forgot to tick is the one you needed apart. Could be added to 2A later as "Only these apps apart". Not mocked further.

3 · Using the tracks

In your editor, and in Neria

3A · Named tracks in the file, and a Tracks row in the clipper's Sound PICK

Your editor (Premiere, DaVinci, CapCut): each track arrives named ("VALORANT", "Discord", "Mic") because the names are written into the MP4. In Neria: opening a replay in the clipper shows its tracks in Clip → Sound, each with a mute and a volume, and the export mixes them as set (track 1 is rebuilt from the others).
