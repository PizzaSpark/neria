Present with sound

Today Present in a window opens scrcpy's window with no sound, while the in-app picture keeps running next to it. Sharing that window in Discord with sound sends silence, because Discord takes a window's sound from its own program. Below: the window carries the phone's sound, the page steps aside while it shows, and one sound choice covers Mirror, Sound only and Present, so the phone's volume buttons behave the same everywhere. Picks are marked PICK.

1 · The page while presenting

One picture at a time

Checked on a POCO X7 Pro (Android 16): Neria and the window can both take the phone's sound at the same time, so Clip the last 30 s keeps its sound while you present. Neria stops playing the sound itself, or you'd hear it twice.

1A · The picture steps aside, everything else stays PICK

Where the phone was: a dashed card, Showing in its own window, with Bring it to front. The status line says Presenting POCO X7 Pro. Clip, Record and Screenshot still work (they come from Neria's own copy). Stop presenting brings the picture back; closing the window by hand does the same.

3 · The phone's sound

One choice for Mirror, Sound only and Present

Today Mirror mutes the phone and follows its volume buttons, while Sound only keeps the phone playing and sends full level here (in that mode Android hands Neria each app's sound before the phone's volume). Same app, two behaviours, and nothing on screen says why.

3A · Two plain choices, shared by all three PICK

Replaces "Keep playing on the phone too" (Sound only) and Mirror's fixed behaviour. On this PC only is the default: the phone's buttons work as people expect, also for what viewers hear. Neria's Volume slider matters only with the second choice, so it's dimmed with the first. Saved like the other phone settings.
