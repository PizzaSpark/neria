Queue filters and flagged songs

Two questions. Filters: a 40-song album shouldn't bury the one download that failed. The filters are All · Active (waiting, downloading, paused) · Done · Needs a look (failed, or a song whose match is in doubt). Each option is shown wide and at the 400px compact width. Flags: today every doubtful Spotify song gets the same amber “Check” tag, whether it's a small doubt or clearly the wrong recording.

Question 1

Where do the filters go?

A · Tabs in the queue header

Calm text tabs with counts, where “Queue” is today. Costs no extra row on wide windows. Reads like a list you're looking at, not a control; on compact the tabs get their own line.

C · Problems come to you

No filter row until something needs you. Then a soft amber strip at the top of the queue says what and offers “Show them” (which filters to just those, with a way back). Active/Done stay as a small switch. Quietest when all is well; the most “UX first”, but Active/Done is less prominent.

Question 2

Flagged songs

Both options split the flag in two: “Unsure” (amber: title match was weak, or the sound is close but maybe another edit) and “Sounds different” (red: the audio check says it's another recording). Both add Play (opens the file so you can listen) and It's right (clears the flag, and it stays cleared).

1 · On the row

Rows stay one line. The reason is the status line; Pick match is the main button; Play and It's right sit next to it on wide windows and go in the ⋯ menu on compact.

2 · A strip under the row

A flagged row opens a small strip that explains the doubt in a full sentence and puts all three answers in reach, on every width. Taller, but hard to miss and nothing hides in a menu.
