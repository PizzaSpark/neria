"Clip that": your voice presses save

The listener works (on your mic: 6 of 6 heard, nothing in between, ~2–3% of one core). This is the part you see: where you turn it on and type your phrase, how Test it makes sure it hears you (and finds a spelling for a made-up word like "Neria"), and what happens in the live player when it hears you. Capture's own screen comes later and reuses all of this.

Ground rules

What it does and doesn't

  • Off until you turn it on. The first time, it fetches a 5 MB voice model; nothing at first run.
  • Listens only while something can be clipped: the live player is open (watching or recording a stream), and later while Capture runs. Not "always while Neria is open". A Listening mark is on screen the whole time, and clicking it stops listening.
  • Never records the mic or sends it anywhere. The phrase is matched on the PC and the sound is thrown away.
  • Hearing it = the same as pressing the button: while recording, "Clip the last 30 s"; while only watching, "Save the last 30 s". The length is the one setting it has (30 s by default).

2 · Test it

Making sure it hears you, and your word

The listener only knows how a word is spelled, not how you say it. Common words are fine ("clip that", "save it"); a name like "Neria" needs a spelling that sounds right ("Nearia" was heard, "Neria" wasn't). Test it finds that for you instead of asking you to guess.

2A · Right in the section, three boxes PICK

Three boxes fill as you speak; the meter shows the mic is alive. If it misses, Neria quietly tries a few spellings that sound the same against what you just said (it kept the sound for this test only, then throws it away) and offers the one that works. Nothing changes until you press Use it.

3 · In the live player

Knowing it listens, and that it heard you

3A · A Listening mark in the bar, a toast when it hears you PICK

The mark sits with the other controls, a small level so you can see it's your mic; click it to stop listening for this stream. On hearing the phrase: the "saved" sound, a pop-yellow flash round the picture, the new part on the tape, and a toast that says what happened with Undo (a false alarm costs one click).

3B · Just the sound and the new clip, no mark

Quieter, but nothing on screen says the mic is being listened to, and a false alarm only shows up later as an extra clip in the queue. Fails the "you always know it's listening" rule. Not mocked further.

Watching (not recording), and when it's off

Watching: the same mark, and hearing it saves the last 30 s ("Saved the last 30 s · Show"). Voice off in Settings: no mark at all. Mic gone (unplugged): the mark turns to Mic not found and clicking it opens Settings → Voice.
