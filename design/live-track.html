Recording live streams

Today a live link on the Download page just starts yt-dlp: no word that it's live, a bar stuck at 0, and Cancel throws the recording away (there's no Stop that keeps it). This track makes a live link a recording: it says where it'll start from, shows what's been caught, and Stop saves the file. Three calls to make, each wide and at 400 px. Numbers are from real runs on 2026-11-15 (Sky News, lofi radio, a Twitch stream).

So a 24/7 radio that's been live for 11 days can't sensibly start "from the start". The proposed rule: from the start when the stream began under 6 hours ago and the site keeps it, otherwise from now. Either way you can switch before pressing Record.

Question 2

The recording's row in the queue

The row has to answer: is it still recording, how much do I have, did I miss anything. The bar is the stream from its start to now: yellow = recorded, striped yellow = still catching up, hatched = before you joined, red ticks = parts missed (connection dropped), the red edge pulses at "now".

A · The tape, stats on one line

The whole bar is the timeline, with times under it. One stats line; the rest (bitrate, pieces, retries) in the row's ⋯ → "Details".

Behaviour

Stop keeps everything recorded so far as one file; Cancel (⋯ menu) throws it away and asks first. The stream ending saves it too. Closing Neria while recording asks ("1 recording is running: stop and save it?"); after a crash the pieces are saved at the next start. A dropped connection reconnects by itself and the gap shows as a red tick. A recording doesn't take a download slot, so other downloads keep going.
