Capture: a Quality choice instead of knobs

OBS asks for rate control, CBR, keyframe interval and a bit rate. Neria asks one thing: how good should it look, and how big may it get. There are three choices, Smaller files, Standard and Best, on both Capture tabs (This PC and Phone). Neria works out the size, frame rate and bit rate for what's being recorded. An Advanced fold under them has the three as numbers (and the Encoder chips, moved there) for anyone who wants them.

Ground rules

What the choice does

  • Standard is what records today (the screen's own size, 60 fps, 16 Mbit/s; the phone at its own size, 60 fps, 8 Mbit/s), so nothing changes for anyone who never touches it.
  • Each choice says what it costs, in MB a minute, worked out for what's picked now (a 1440p screen and a 1080p one get different bit rates for the same choice: Neria scales by pixels × frame rate).
  • Smaller files scales down on the graphics card (scale_d3d11, in our ffmpeg) so the game still hardly notices. Best never scales up.
  • No rate-control words anywhere. Neria keeps its variable bit rate with a cap (what it does now). CBR only matters for streaming, and pushing a phone's encoder to it makes some phones stall.
  • Changing it while it runs applies at the next quiet restart (a second or two, like Never record); the minute kept so far stays as it was.

2 · Advanced

The numbers, for anyone who wants them

2A · Size, frame rate and bit rate, then the encoder PICK

Open, it shows what the picked choice set. Changing any number picks Custom (a fourth row appears with what you set) and the changed control gets the "set" outline. Back to Standard undoes it. The Encoder chips move in here from the main column: hardly anyone needs them, and Automatic already picks the fastest one that works. Frame rate only offers what the screen can show (120 on a 120 Hz+ screen).

3 · Phone

The same three on the Phone tab

3A · Under "While mirroring", worded for a phone PICK

The phone takes only size, frame rate and bit rate (scrcpy's options), so Advanced has those three. Smaller files is also the one for slow Wi-Fi. If Best makes the phone's picture stall (some encoders can't), Neria steps back to Standard and says so in amber, instead of leaving a frozen mirror. It applies to the mirror, its replay buffer and Record; Present keeps its own lighter picture, since it's already a second stream over the same Wi-Fi.
