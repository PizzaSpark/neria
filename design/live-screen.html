One live screen: watch, record, clip

Today a live stream is split across three places: the Download page decides from the start / from now before you've seen anything, Record drops a row in the queue, and watching or clipping is a separate trip (the unlabelled ▶ on the thumbnail, or Watch on the row). And when the recording stops, the player keeps a dead Start clip and a Stop recording that does nothing.

This revises 1A of live-track.html (the start point said on the preview). The queue row (2A) and "Clip the last…" (3A) stay; they become the background view of what this screen shows.

The flow

Paste → Watch or Record → the same live screen

  • Watch and Record both land on the live screen (the Clip page in live mode). Watch just plays; Record plays and records.
  • On that screen you can start or stop recording any time, and clip whether or not you record.
  • The start point (now / from the start) moves off the preview and onto the screen, next to Record.
  • Leaving the screen never stops a recording: it carries on as a row in the queue, and Watch on the row brings you back.

Question 1

Where "from now / from the start" goes

It has to be a real choice, not a sentence with a link. Three ways:

A · In Record's menu simplest

Record = from now (instant, clipping works right away). The ▾ offers the other one with its cost. Same split button that's already there.

Start recording…
From nowstarts this second
From the start2 h 19 min back · ~23 min to catch up

B · A two-way switch next to Record

Always visible before you record, so nobody misses that "from the start" exists. Costs a slot in the bar (at 400 px it takes its own line).

Not recording · you can clip the last 10 min From nowFrom the start~23 min ◉ Record

C · Always from now, then "get the earlier part too" recommended, needs a check

Record never makes you choose or wait. While it records, a link offers the part before you pressed it; Neria fetches that in the background and joins it on Stop (the parts already join without re-encoding). Needs a check that both can run at once on YouTube and Twitch; if not, it falls back to A.
