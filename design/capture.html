Capture: instant replay, with a face

The engine already keeps the last minute of a game or a monitor on the GPU, with separate sound tracks, and F8 saves it. This is the part you see: where it lives, turning it on, what it records (a game, a window or a monitor by name), how much, the sound tracks and the key; the status while it runs; the toast when a clip is saved (in the window and as a Windows notification, since the game is usually in front), the tray, and "clip that" by voice.

Ground rules

What it does and doesn't

  • Off until you turn it on. Turning it on is the page's one yellow action; it stays on (also from the tray) until you turn it off. Not on at start-up unless you ask ("Turn on when Neria starts").
  • Nothing is saved until you press the key (or say the phrase). The kept minute lives in pieces next to the exe that overwrite the oldest, and are deleted when it stops.
  • A game that isn't open yet is fine: it waits for its window ("Waiting for Valorant to open") and starts by itself, then waits again when the game closes.
  • Saved clips go to Videos (the same folder as downloads), named "Valorant 2026-11-21 21-04 (last 1m00s).mp4", and open in the clipper with one click.
1 · Where it lives

A Capture page, or a Settings section

1A · A fourth page, "Capture", in the rail PICK

Download, Clip, Compress, Capture. It's a thing you turn on and watch, not a preference, and it has a list of what you saved. The rail's Capture icon gets a small red dot while it runs, so you see it from every page. On compact windows the tab bar gets a fifth tab (five fit at 400: 78 px each).

1B · A "Capture" section in Settings, with a strip on the Download page

Fewer places, but you'd turn it on from a drawer, the saved clips have no home except the queue, and the status would be a strip squeezed above the link box. Turning a recorder on and off is not a setting. Not mocked further.

2 · What to record

Games first, then windows, then monitors by name

2A · One list, games on top PICK

Programs with a window open now, a Game tag when it covers a whole screen or lives in a games folder (Steam, Epic, Riot, Battle.net, Xbox), then every monitor by its own name and size ("DELL U2720Q · Main screen · 2560 × 1440"). The picked game stays in the list when it closes ("not open now"), so it waits for it. Other windows (browsers, Explorer) only under "Show every open window".

2B · Three chips (Game / Window / Monitor), then a menu

Two clicks for the common case and the menu hides the names. Not mocked further.

3 · Saved

Knowing it saved, without leaving the game

3A · The "saved" sound + a Windows notification; a toast when Neria is in front PICK

In a game you can't see Neria, so the key plays the short "saved" sound (as now) and Windows shows a notification with Open in clipper (the notification setting in General applies; off: the sound only). When Neria's window is in front, its own toast says it. Voice: the same, plus "undo that" deletes it.

4 · Tray

Turning it on and off without the window

4A · Two lines added to the tray menu PICK

While instant replay is on, closing the window goes to the tray without asking (a recorder that quits when you close it would surprise you). The tray's tooltip says the status line.
