Download track: the visual choices

Mocks for the Download feature track (ROADMAP items 1–12), dark theme. Each question has 2–3 options; the one marked “pick” is my recommendation. Items without a real visual choice (queue reordering) just follow the design system.

Item 1

Signing in to a site

When a link needs an account (private, age-restricted, members-only, Instagram's limit), the preview says so. Neria opens the site's own sign-in page in a small window of its own (Windows' built-in Edge WebView2, nothing to install), keeps that site's cookies in Neria's folder, and only ever hands them to yt-dlp for links on that same site.

A · “Sign in to YouTube” right on the problem pick

The problem preview gets one button. The window has a calm Neria bar on top: what's happening, and Done. Neria notices when you're signed in and says so. Back in Neria the link is looked up again by itself. Settings → Network lists signed-in sites with Sign out, plus “Import cookies.txt”.

Items 2–4

Captions, audio tracks and chapters on the preview

Three pickers that only appear when there is something to pick (captions exist, several audio tracks, chapters). One shared pattern for all three.

Under the quality chips: small ghost buttons that say the current choice. Each opens the shared Dialog with search, groups (“Made by the creator” / “Auto-generated”), ticks, and its options (SRT / VTT / ASS / text; separate · in the video · both; “Captions only”). On compact the line wraps.

Item 5

Closing with downloads running, notifications, “Download later”

A · Ask once when it matters pick

Closing while something is downloading asks: “Keep going in the tray” / “Stop and quit”, with “Don't ask again”. Settings: When I close Neria: Ask · Keep going in the tray · Quit. Closing with nothing running just quits. “Download later” sits on a small ▾ beside Download. Windows notifications with the thumbnail, “Open” and “Show in folder”.

B · Tray is a plain setting

“Keep running in the tray when closed”, off by default, no question. Simple, but people lose downloads by closing before they find it.

C · Always to the tray

Like Discord/Steam: close = hide, “Quit” from the tray menu. Downloads never get lost; some people find it annoying.

Best quality · Compatible (H.264 MP4, plays everywhere) · Smallest file · Audio only. A quiet “Advanced” link folds out the old resolution chips and the container right there.

Item 8

A · A dialog from Settings → Download pick

Settings shows “File names: Title [ID] · Change”. The dialog: presets as chips, the line you build (drag field chips in, type text between them, / makes a folder), the fields to drag from, a live example from the current link, warnings, and the raw template under Advanced.

Item 9

Several links, “Paste & download”, the clipboard watcher

A · A list in the preview; watcher as a calm strip pick

Pasting several links (or dropping a .txt) lists them like the playlist picker, each with its title as it loads; duplicates are greyed. “Paste & download” is on the Download ▾ menu (and Ctrl+Shift+V). The watcher (off by default) shows a strip at the top when you copy a link while Neria is open; from the tray it sends a notification instead.

B · Each link becomes a queue row straight away

No list step: rows appear waiting and look themselves up. Faster, but you can't untick anything first.

Item 10

Queue order (no real choice, for reference)

Waiting rows get a drag handle, “Move to top / bottom” in the row menu, and Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓. Running rows stay put.

Item 11

Watch the link before downloading

A · Play on the thumbnail → the player page pick

A yellow play mark on the preview thumbnail. It opens the same player as Clip first, without In/Out: play, seek, volume, and “Download” / “Clip this”. Back returns to the preview. Reuses the tested player.

Item 12

Follow a channel or playlist

A · “Follow” on the preview, list in Settings pick

Channel and playlist previews get “Follow” next to Download. Settings → Following lists them: how often (hourly · daily · weekly), last check, Pause, Unfollow, “Check now”. New uploads arrive as a group card in the queue. Checks run while Neria runs (tray included); a missed check runs at start-up. Following gets only new uploads.
