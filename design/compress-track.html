Compress track: goals you can understand

Roadmap items 1–5 under "Compress". Three ways to pick the goal (A, B, C), each at 900 and 400 wide, then one shared treatment for the rest: the before / after, Advanced, several files, and trimming. Click the goals: the hints and numbers are worked out the way the app would, for a 2:14 1080p60 clip of 312 MB.

How the hints work

From the real plan for that file: the height it'll be (against the original) and how much room the bitrate has over what the format needs at that size. Plenty of room at the original size → looks the same; some → nearly the same; just enough → a bit softer; downscaled under 480p → soft, under 360p → blurry. "Keeping it" goals have no fixed size, so Neria encodes three 2-second samples with the real settings a moment after you pick (about a second with a graphics card) to say "about 48 MB". The same samples give the after frame. The estimate never blocks Compress.
