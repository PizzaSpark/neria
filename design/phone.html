Phone: mirror, record and clip your Android phone

scrcpy's picture is excellent; getting it running is the hard part (Developer options, adb, IPs and ports) and it stops at a plain window. Neria's take: the phone is another capture source, next to instant replay, feeding the clipper and Compress. Not a phone manager. Below: where it lives, connecting by QR, the mirror itself, Sound only and Present. Recommended picks are marked PICK.

1 · Where it lives

Next to instant replay, or a page of its own

1A · The Capture page gets two tabs: "This PC" and "Phone" PICK

Both are a source you turn on, with the same Clip the last…, the same Saved list and the same toast with Open in clipper. The rail stays at five places (the tab bar at 400 px stays roomy), and the Capture icon's red dot means "something is being kept", from the PC or the phone. Calm filter-style tabs, as in the queue: they change the view, not a setting. The phone's tab shows its name once one is connected ("Pixel 7").

2 · Connecting

A QR code, not IPs and ports

Android 11+ pairs over Wi-Fi by scanning a QR code in Developer options → Wireless debugging → Pair device with QR code (what Android Studio does). Neria shows the code, sees the phone on the network, pairs and connects; a paired phone reconnects by itself afterwards (adb does that for phones it knows). The one thing we can't skip: turning on Developer options once. Steps tick themselves off as Neria notices them.

2A · QR on the left, the steps with pictures on the right PICK

Everything on one screen, the code always visible (the phone camera wants it while you're in Settings). A pairing code and USB are quiet links underneath. The steps are calm rows; the current one has a yellow outline; done ones turn green.

3 · Mirroring

The phone in Neria, with its own replay

3A · Picture on the left, actions on the right (as in 1A) PICK

The phone's picture at its own shape; a landscape game turns the picture and the actions move under it. Clip the last 30 s is the yellow one (the buffer keeps 1 min, like Capture's). 5 · Present the phone

Only the phone, in a window of its own

5A · A bare window named "Phone screen · Pixel 7" PICK

No Neria around it, no bars: easy to pick in Discord, Teams, Meet or OBS, or to put on a projector. It keeps the phone's shape when resized, turns when the phone turns, and you can still use the phone in it. Choices next to the button: Stay on top, Show taps (people see what you press), Keep the phone awake. The page shows "Presenting" with Stop; clipping keeps working.

How it works

What's under it

PieceHow
Toolsscrcpy's own Windows release (Apache 2.0), one pinned version, fetched into bin/phone/ the first time you open the Phone tab (11 MB: adb, scrcpy, its server). Never at first run.
Connectingadb's QR pairing (mDNS) as Android Studio does; Neria's own adb on its own port, so Android Studio's adb is never killed.
The picturescrcpy's server on the phone streams H.264 and the sound to Neria; Neria shows it with its own player and sends taps, keys and scrolling back.
Clip the last…The same short pieces as instant replay (copied, not encoded again), so clipping costs nothing.
Not portable, one thingadb keeps its key and the list of paired phones in your user folder (.android), as Android Studio does; adb can't be told to keep them elsewhere.
