Command palette: Ctrl+K, type, Enter

Neria has grown to five pages, a Settings drawer and a dozen dialogs. Most of it is a few clicks away, but you have to know where: Make a GIF is on the Clip page, Transcribe is in Extras or a dropped file's choice, "Add lyrics to songs I already have" is in Settings → Spotify. The palette is one box that reaches all of it: press Ctrl+K anywhere, type a few letters of what you want to do or of something you saved, press Enter. It adds no new features; it's a faster way to the ones there are, and to the Library's things.

Chosen 2026-11-23: 1A, 2A, 3A + 3B, 4A, 5A (the PICKs below).

Ground rules

What it is, and what it isn't

  • It goes where the thing already is. "Compress" opens the Compress page with the file in it, "Settings: Voice" opens that section, a saved video opens in the player. Nothing happens in the palette itself, so there's nothing new to learn: you land on the same screen a click would have taken you to.
  • Two kinds of rows: things to do and things you saved. Actions (Download a link, Clip a file, Make a GIF, Turn on instant replay, Settings sections…) and the Library's records (by title, site or file name).
  • The keyboard is enough. Ctrl+K opens it, typing filters, ↑ ↓ move, Enter does it, Esc closes. The mouse works too. It remembers what you pick, so the things you use come first next time.
  • Words you'd say, not our labels. "extract audio", "mp3", "make smaller", "shrink", "discord" all find Compress; "gif" finds Make a GIF; "record" finds Record a live stream and instant replay. Each action has a few such words (the table at the end).
  • It's never in the way. No button grows on a page for it except one small entry point (section 5), and on compact it's the same box as a sheet.
1 · How it opens

A box over the page, or a field that drops down

Where the box appears decides how it feels: a separate moment ("I'm telling Neria something") or part of the page's header.

1A · A box in the middle, over the dimmed page PICK

Like Raycast or VS Code. The page stays where it was behind it; Esc puts you back. The same box on every page, so it's always in the same place. Typing goes straight in (the box has the keyboard).

2 · Before you type

What the empty box shows

2A · A copied link, a few recent things, a few common actions PICK

As in 1A's picture: the copied link first when the clipboard has one (the clipboard watcher already knows), then the 3 newest things from the Library, then the 3 actions you use most (common ones until it has learnt). Short enough to read at a glance; typing replaces it all with matches.

2B · Every action, grouped by page

A menu of the whole app (Download: 6 · Clip: 9 · Compress: 5 · Capture: 7 · Library: 4 · Settings: 9…). Good for discovering, but 40 rows is a list to scroll, not a box to type in, and it repeats the rail.

3 · Doing something to a file

Thing first, action first, or both

"Compress the VALORANT replay" has two parts. The palette can take them in either order.

3A + 3B · Both ways, each finishing the other PICK

Thing first: type part of its name, then → (or Tab) shows that thing's actions, the same list as its More menu (···) in the Library. Enter on the thing itself does its main action (Watch / Play). Action first: type "compress", Enter: the Library's recent videos to pick from, and "Choose a file…" last for anything else. Either way the second step is the same small list.

3C · Only one order (thing first)

Simpler to build, but "compress" would only open an empty Compress page: the most common way people think ("I want to compress…") would stop halfway.

4 · While typing

Mixed results, or actions and things apart

4A · Two groups, actions above things, 5 each at most PICK

The groups never mix, so a row's place says what Enter will do: go to a page or a dialog ("Do"), or open something you saved ("Saved"). A group with no matches doesn't show ("tokyo" only finds Saved). A link pasted into the box becomes "Download this link" at the top.

5 · How you find out it exists

One small way in, and compact

5A · A search button at the top of the rail; on compact, next to the gear PICK

The rail gets a small button above the pages (magnifier, "Ctrl K" under it); clicking it opens the box. On compact (400 px) the tab bar keeps its five pages and the header gets a magnifier next to the Settings gear; the box opens as a sheet from the top, the keyboard up.

5B · Keyboard only, mentioned once

Nothing on screen; a one-time tip ("Press Ctrl+K to do anything from anywhere") after the first few downloads, and a line in Settings → About. Cleanest, but most people would never find it, and compact has no keyboard.
