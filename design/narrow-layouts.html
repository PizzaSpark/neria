Narrow Neria: three ways to fold it up

Every mock is the narrowest supported window, 400 × 700. That's a Windows window snapped to a quarter or a third of the screen, and almost exactly an Android phone (360–412 wide), so whichever you pick is also the Android layout later. Above 720px wide nothing changes much from today; the big decisions are only down here.

Wide · 1100+Today's layout: 80px rail, quality chips beside the preview, labelled row actions, column capped at 1240.
Medium · 720–1099Rail stays. Chips get their own row; Clip / Compress on rows shrink to icons. Settings drawer as today.
Compact · under 720The new layout below. Window minimum: 400 × 560 (today it's 780 × 580).
Question 1

Where does the navigation go?

Same Download page in all three, same queue rows; only the navigation moves. The 80px rail would eat a fifth of a 400px window, so it has to change.

A · Bottom tabs

Android's own pattern, so it carries over as is. Pages get the full width; the logo moves next to the headline. Costs 64px of height at the bottom.

Question 2

Queue row buttons

A done video has Show, Clip, Compress and ×; a failed one Retry, Report and ×. At 400px they no longer fit next to the title. Two ways out (shown with bottom tabs, works with any nav):

1 · Buttons underneath

Everything stays one tap away, labelled, under the status line. Rows get taller (about 96px instead of 62), so fewer fit on screen.

Settings, Clip and Compress

These follow from the rules in DESIGN.md, so I'd do them this way whatever you pick above (tell me if something looks off). Settings becomes a full-window sheet with the sections as chips along the top. Clip stacks: player, controls, timeline, then a pinned Export bar; the keyboard-shortcut line hides (no keyboard on a phone). Compress puts each option's label above its chips, chips wrap, Compress stays pinned at the bottom.
