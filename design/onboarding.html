First run and empty pages

What a new user sees right after setup, and what Clip and Compress show before you open anything. Today: an empty queue, and big centred icons with a title (the “giant centred Nothing here yet” that DESIGN.md calls a vibecoded tell).

Question 1

The very first Download page

Shown until the first download starts (then never again). Both teach the same three things: paste or drag a link, pick a quality, and that Spotify links and Clip-before-download exist.

A · Three steps in the empty queue

Calm numbered rows where the queue will be (same look as queue rows), plus a “Hide tips” link. Teaches the whole flow; disappears by itself once there's a download.
