**Unzip anywhere and run Neria.exe.** The first start downloads yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, mpv and gallery-dl into a `bin` folder next to it; nothing is installed on your PC. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't signed yet: More info → Run anyway. Already on 0.7? Neria offers the update itself.

This release makes live streams one place: watch, record and clip in the player.

### One live screen
- A live link's preview has **Watch** and **Record**. Record starts recording from now and opens the player on it, so you can clip straight away.
- In the player: **Start clip** / **End clip**, **Save the last** 30 s to 10 min, and **Stop**. When it's saved: **Show file**, **Trim it** or **Record again**.
- Just watching? **Save the last** works there too, and **Record** is one press away. On Twitch, Neria keeps the last 10 minutes by itself while you watch, so the moment you want is already there.

### Get the part before
- Recording from now, and want what came before you pressed Record? **Also get the 2 h 19 min before**: Neria fetches it next to the recording and puts it in front when you stop, joined where the sound matches. YouTube and Twitch.

### Twitch without its ads
- Recordings and the player no longer start with Twitch's "Commercial break in progress": Neria takes the stream from Twitch players that come without the ad, and switches if one starts showing it. Signed in to Twitch, nothing changes.
- Twitch recordings no longer claim a wrong length in other players.

### Missed bits, got back
- If your connection drops or slows while recording, Neria fetches the missed stretch again from the site once it's steady, and splices it in where it belongs ("got back 0:12 missed"). Works where the site keeps the stream: YouTube (the last hour while live, all of it after), Twitch broadcasts that aren't for subscribers only.

### Smaller things
- Download page: the format button at the right now opens the Advanced options (no more Advanced link in the middle); "Any" quality is now **Highest (2160p)**, with the video's own best; the greyed Download button lines up with Paste.
- Compress page: a size still being worked out says "working…".
