**Unzip anywhere and run Neria.exe.** The first start downloads yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, mpv and gallery-dl into a `bin` folder next to it; nothing is installed on your PC. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't signed yet: More info → Run anyway. Already on 0.3? Neria offers the update itself.

### Signed-in downloads
- **Sign in to a site inside Neria** for private, age-restricted and members-only videos, Instagram without its limit, and your own Liked / Watch later lists. A problem that signing in fixes shows "Sign in to YouTube" (or the site); a small window opens on the site's own sign-in page.
- Each site's sign-in stays in Neria's folder and is only ever sent to that site. Sign out in Settings → Network, or import a `cookies.txt`.

### More from each video
- **Captions**: pick languages (the creator's own or auto-generated), as SRT, VTT, ASS or plain text, saved next to the video, inside it, or both. "Captions only" skips the video.
- **Audio tracks**: videos dubbed in several languages let you keep one or several; the original is marked.
- **Chapters**: download only the chapters you tick, as separate files or joined into one. The clipper shows chapter marks too.
- **Watch it first**: a play button on the preview streams the video before you decide.

### Easier downloading
- **Presets** instead of codec talk: Best quality · Compatible (plays everywhere) · Smallest file · Audio only. The old options live under Advanced.
- **Several links at once**: paste them all, pick which ones. "Paste & download" (Ctrl+Shift+V) skips the preview, and an optional clipboard watcher offers links you copy elsewhere.
- **Filename builder**: drag fields like Title, Channel or Date into a name, no template syntax.
- **Queue order**: drag waiting downloads into the order you want, or move them to the top.
- **Follow** a channel or playlist and Neria downloads its new uploads by itself.
- **Download later**: tonight at 02:00, tomorrow morning, or a time you pick.
- **Keeps going in the tray** when you close the window, with a notification when things finish or fail.
- In playlists and posts, drag items into your own order, reverse newest-first lists, and pick a format per item.

### Spotify and lyrics
- **Synced lyrics** from LRCLIB for far more songs: a `.lrc` file next to each song and the words inside it. Each song's row now says what it found: synced, plain text only, or none.
- **Spotify's own synced lyrics** (Musixmatch), optional and off by default: sign in to Spotify in Settings → Spotify → Lyrics. This is against Spotify's terms and could get the account restricted, so use a spare account. It's used for lyrics only, never audio.
- **Lyrics only**: next to a Spotify album or playlist, save just the lyrics (`.lrc` and `.txt`), named like the songs.
- **Add lyrics to songs I already have**: pick a folder and Neria adds lyrics to every song in it.
- **Number playlist songs** (`01 Artist - Title`, optional), so the folder sorts in playlist order. Songs you already have are renamed, not downloaded again.
- Settings → Spotify → Lyrics lets you choose the sources and "Synced lyrics only".

### Smaller things
- In narrow windows the link box keeps its full width; Download moves to its own row underneath.
- Fixes found by testing on real links: joined chapters kept only one part; captions inside the video left extra files behind and didn't reach webm files; "Smallest file" sometimes picked a dubbed language instead of the video's own.
