**Unzip anywhere and run Neria.exe.** The first start downloads yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, mpv and gallery-dl into a `bin` folder next to it; nothing is installed on your PC. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't signed yet: More info → Run anyway. Already on 0.9? Neria offers the update itself.

A polish release: Capture's settings where you'd look for them, your graphics card for clips too, and a calmer, steadier app to move around in.

### Capture settings, OBS-style
- **Settings → Capture** has everything instant replay records with: how much to keep, Quality, Encoder, the sound tracks and mic, Never record, the save key and turning on at start. The Capture page shows the same choices, and changing one changes both.
- **Encoder** (Automatic / NVIDIA / AMD / Intel / CPU) is no longer hidden under Quality → Advanced. It has its own heading.

### Your graphics card for clips too
- **Settings → General → Video work on**: Automatic, Graphics card or Processor.
- Clips cut exactly, Sound and Picture changes on a video, GIFs saved as MP4, and chat or captions burned in now use your graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel). Before, they always used the processor. Compress, workflows and speech to text follow the same choice.
- If the card can't make a file, Neria makes it on the processor instead, so a clip is never lost.

### Speech to text, explained
- Settings → Speech to text names **Whisper** and lists what's on your PC: the **Fast** model (large-v3-turbo), the **Best quality** model (large-v3) and the engine, each with its size.
- Remove either model on its own while the other stays. Picking it again later downloads it again.

### Smoother to use
- The Settings drawer slides in and out, the open section's highlight slides along the list, and a section fades in when picked.
- Switching pages is a quick fade, and every page now starts at the same place, so nothing jumps sideways.
- **File names**: dragging a piece of the name really moves it under your mouse, with a mark where it will land. Fields dragged up from below land where you drop them.
- Dialogs, the drawer and the palette close with Esc or a click outside. Focus goes back to what opened them, and editors with unsaved changes ask before throwing them away. Tabs, chips and menus work with the arrow keys.

### Fixes
- Settings scrolls all the way down. The end of a long section, such as Speech to text, was cut off.
- The Capture page's Encoder chips no longer overlap the Sound heading when they wrap.
- A stray box character in Speech to text's description is gone.
