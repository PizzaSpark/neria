**Unzip anywhere and run Neria.exe.** The first start downloads yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, mpv and gallery-dl into a `bin` folder next to it; nothing is installed on your PC. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't signed yet: More info → Run anyway. Already on 0.6? Neria offers the update itself.

This release is all about **recording live streams**.

### Paste a live link, press Record
- A live stream says so on its preview, and where the recording will start: **from the start** ("about 22 min to catch up, then it follows live") or **from now** (and why: it's been on for days, or the stream can't be rewound). One click switches between the two.
- From the start works on YouTube and Twitch: Neria catches up at several times real speed, then keeps following live.
- A stream that hasn't started yet: **Record when it starts**. Neria waits and records from the first second.

### See what you've got
- The row shows the stream from its start to now: what's recorded, the part before you joined, anything missed ("missed 0:42 at 31:05") and a pulsing live edge.
- How long, how big, the picture size, and while catching up how long until it's live.
- If the connection drops, Neria reconnects by itself.

### Stop keeps it
- **Stop** saves everything recorded so far as one file. The stream ending saves it too. Cancel asks before throwing anything away.
- Recordings don't hold up your other downloads, and Pause all leaves them alone.
- Closing Neria asks: keep recording in the tray, or stop, save and quit. If Neria crashes, the recording is saved the next time it starts.

### Watch it and clip it as it happens
- **Watch** plays the stream live while it records.
- **Start clip** when something good begins (it starts 10 s back, so you don't lose the first words) and **End clip** when it's over: that part is saved as its own file while the recording goes on.
- Or **Clip the last** 30 s, 1 min, 5 min or 10 min, right away.
