**Unzip anywhere and run Neria.exe.** The first start downloads yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, mpv and gallery-dl into a `bin` folder next to it; nothing is installed on your PC. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't signed yet: More info → Run anyway. Already on 0.8? Neria offers the update itself.

The biggest release yet: instant replay of your games, your phone on your PC, a Library of everything you've saved, live chat, speech to text, and much more for sound files.

### Capture: instant replay
- A new **Capture** page keeps the last minutes of a game or your screen, and **F8** saves them from anywhere, even inside the game. A toast says it's saved, with **Open in clipper**.
- **Automatic** records the game in front. Screens and programs are picked by name ("DELL U2720Q", the game's own name).
- **A sound track per app**: the game, Discord and your mic each get their own track, with "Everything" first. **Never record** keeps an app out completely. In the clipper, **Sound → Tracks** turns each one on or off and sets its volume.
- **Quality**: Smaller files, Standard or Best, worked out from your screen. Recording runs on your graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), with the CPU as the fallback.

### Say "clip that"
- Type any phrase, and Neria listens for it on your mic while instant replay runs or the live player is open. Saying it saves the last part, and "undo that" takes it back.
- **Test it** in Settings → Voice. Your own voice samples help when the phrase isn't heard, accents included.

### Your phone on your PC
- Capture page → **Phone**: connect an Android phone by QR code or cable, and its screen shows in Neria at full size. Tap, scroll and type on it.
- **Clip the last 30 s**, **Record** and **Screenshot**, as for a game. **Sound only** plays just its sound.
- **Present** puts the phone in a window of its own, with its sound, ready to share on a call.
- Pull out the cable and mirroring carries on over Wi-Fi (when the phone is on Wi-Fi too).
- The phone tools are downloaded only when you first use this.

### The Library
- A fifth page with **everything you've saved**: downloads, clips, recordings, replays, compressed copies, GIFs, transcripts and songs. Arranged by day, with tabs per kind, and Site, Date, Sort and search.
- Every row has its actions: Watch, Clip, Compress, Make a GIF, Transcribe, Copy file, Copy link, Download again…
- Files you moved or deleted say **Gone**, and **Find it…** finds them again (with the others from the same folder). A **Duplicates** tab shows anything saved twice.
- **Pick several** (Ctrl/Shift+click) to convert, even out, tag or put them in folders all at once.

### Command palette
- **Ctrl+K** finds any action by its own words, anything in your Library, and what you can do with a copied link.

### Sound files
- **Compress takes sound files**: Plays everywhere (M4A), MP3, Smallest (Opus), Lossless (FLAC) or For editing (WAV). A video can become **Sound only**.
- The trim strip shows the **waveform**. Keep **several parts**, **Even out** the volume (one song or a whole album), and **Cut the silence** at the start and end.
- **Edit tags** for one song or many: Number in order, and **Find tags online**.
- **Put in folders**: Artist / Album from the tags, lyrics moved along, playlists rewritten. **Undo** puts everything back.
- Clip page: **Paste times…** turns timestamps from a description into named clips.

### Speech to text
- **Transcribe** a video or sound file into text or subtitles (.srt), on your own PC. Pick **Fast** or **Best quality** (Best also translates). The model downloads only once you ask for it.

### Live chat
- A live stream's chat shows beside the player, Twitch and YouTube alike, with their emotes. It's saved next to recordings, and replays with past streams.
- **Chat in this clip**: leave it out, keep it as a track, or burn it in beside or over the picture.

### Workflows and your own tools
- **Workflows** say what happens to a file after it's saved, like making it smaller, keeping only the sound or making a GIF, and where it goes. They can run on every download, on certain sites, on followed channels, or on a folder you watch.
- **Tools**: add your own command-line programs as Neria actions, with blanks for the file, and run them from the Library or the palette.

### Smaller things
- yt-dlp now starts about three times faster: Neria keeps its faster build up to date itself, checked against the release's checksums.
- A Spotify playlist or album you downloaded before shows "N in your folder", with **Download N new** / **Up to date**.
- Quitting never hangs.

Phone mirroring is built on [scrcpy](https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy) by Genymobile. Speech to text uses [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp), and "clip that" uses [sherpa-onnx](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx).
