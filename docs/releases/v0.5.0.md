**Unzip anywhere and run Neria.exe.** The first start downloads yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, mpv and gallery-dl into a `bin` folder next to it; nothing is installed on your PC. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't signed yet: More info → Run anyway. Already on 0.4? Neria offers the update itself.

This release is all about the **Clip** page.

### A timeline you can zoom
- **Zoom in and scroll** on any video: Ctrl+scroll (or pinch on a touchpad) zooms where the pointer is, scrolling moves along, + and − zoom too. Fit clip and Whole video sit under the timeline.
- A thin **overview** of the whole video shows which part is on screen; drag it to move around.
- A **time ruler** that suits the zoom, thin In / Out handles, and the clip's length on a tag you can drag to move the whole clip.
- Zoomed in, the filmstrip gets **real close-up frames** and the waveform stays **sharp at any zoom**. While playing, the view follows the playhead.

### Live streams
- **Rewind a live stream** (YouTube and other sites that keep the last hours): scrub back, mark In and Out, and download just that part. Or press "Save the last 30 s / 1 min / 5 min / 10 min".
- The timeline reads back from **now** (−4:00 … now), with the live edge on the right.
- **Keep the last few minutes** of streams that can't be rewound (Twitch and others), like a replay buffer, and save the last bit whenever something happens. The space it takes stays the same.

### Sound
- **Change the sound when you save a clip**: louder or quieter, even it out (−14 or −16 LUFS for YouTube, TikTok, Discord, podcasts), mute, fade in and out. You hear the level right away.
- **Save the sound only** as MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC or WAV.

### Picture
- **Crop** with a frame you drag, or pick a shape: 9:16 for Shorts and TikTok, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9.
- **Turn and mirror**, and see the result in the player before saving. Phone videos that are recorded sideways now show the right way up.

### GIFs and several clips
- **Make a GIF** from any selection, or a WebP or GIF-like MP4 (much smaller, good for Discord), with the size it'll be shown before you make it. `.gif` files open in the clipper too.
- **Several clips from one video**: add each part to a list, name and reorder them, then save them all at once, as separate files or joined into one. "Add all chapters" in one press.
- Links get a waveform too.

### Smaller things
- **Your own sound** for when downloads finish or fail (Settings → General): pick a short `.wav`; it's copied into Neria's folder so it moves with it.
- In narrower windows the clip page keeps its time readout whole.
