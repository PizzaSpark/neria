**Unzip anywhere and run Neria.exe.** The first start downloads yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, mpv and gallery-dl into a `bin` folder next to it; nothing is installed on your PC. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't signed yet: More info → Run anyway. Already on 0.2? Neria offers the update itself.

### Posts and playlists
- Posts with several photos, videos or GIFs (X, Instagram, Facebook) and playlists list every item under the preview: tick what you want, pick a quality per video, and "Download 3 items".
- Photos in posts are saved too, and every GIF in an X post becomes a real .gif.
- Downloads sort into `Neria\Audio`, `Video` and `Photos`; a playlist or post gets a folder of its own, numbered in order, with a playlist file.
- A playlist is one card in the queue ("3 of 12 saved · 1 failed") that you can open, pause, retry or show.

### A queue you can trust
- **Pause and resume** downloads; they carry on where they stopped.
- The queue **survives closing Neria**: unfinished downloads come back and carry on.
- **Filters**: All · Active · Done · Needs a look (failed, or a song that may be the wrong version).
- Pasting something you already downloaded says so, with "Show file" and "Download again".
- Spotify songs in doubt now say how sure Neria is ("Unsure" or "Sounds different"), with **Play** to listen and **It's right** to keep it.

### Easier to use
- **Drag a link from your browser** onto Neria: it goes into the Download box, or into the clipper on the Clip page. Files still work too.
- Works in **any window size**, down to a narrow strip at the side of your screen (tabs move to the bottom).
- **Keyboard**: Tab through everything, Enter or Space to press, Esc closes dialogs and Settings; the link box is ready for Ctrl+V.
- Clip and Compress list your **recent downloads**, one click to open.
- Settings are now in sections: Download, Spotify, Network, Appearance, About.
- New users get a short "How it works" in the empty queue.
- Better for screen readers and easier to read (contrast checked in both themes).

### Smaller things
- Save the thumbnail as a .jpg next to the video (Settings → Download → Extras).
- The container you pick (mp4/mkv/webm) always applies.
- Clearer messages for posts without a video and for Instagram's limit when you're not signed in.
